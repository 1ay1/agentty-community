## Channel layout

**📢 INFO**
- `#announcements` — Releases and news. github.com/1ay1/agentty
- `#read-me-first` — Start here — what agentty is and how to install it.

**💬 COMMUNITY**
- `#general` — General chat about agentty.
- `#help` — Stuck? Ask here. Include your OS + steps to reproduce.
- `#bug-reports` — Report bugs. Best to also open a GitHub issue.
- `#feature-requests` — Ideas and requests for agentty.
- `#showcase` — Show off cool sessions, workflows, and setups.

## #read-me-first intro

> **agentty** is a native terminal coding agent — one fast binary, no runtime. It brings a flagship model into your terminal with local RAG over your code/docs and Smart Mode routing that learns your repo.
>
> 📖 Docs: https://agentty.org · 💻 Source: https://github.com/1ay1/agentty · ⬇️ Install: https://agentty.org/docs/installation
>
> New here? Say hi in #general, ask in #help, and drop cool sessions in #showcase.

## Rules (paste into #read-me-first or a #rules channel)

> **1. Be kind.** Assume good faith; no harassment, hate, or spam.
> **2. Keep it on-topic** per channel. Support in #help, ideas in #feature-requests.
> **3. Search first.** Your question may already be answered in the docs or history.
> **4. No piracy, malware, or illegal content.**
> **5. English in the main channels** so everyone can follow.
> Breaking these may get your messages removed or you removed.
